ANALYSIS:
============================================================

Zipfian Distribution Explanation:
With a Zipfian-distributed workload (skewness=1.0), only a small
number of keys are accessed frequently ("hot" keys), while most
keys are accessed rarely or never.

Result: LFU outperforms LRU (65.25% vs 57.15%)

Why: LFU explicitly protects genuinely hot keys (high access
frequency) from being evicted by bursts of cold/one-off key
accesses. LRU's weakness: a truly frequently-accessed key can be
evicted if a burst of infrequent keys becomes 'more recent'. LFU
avoids this by tracking actual access frequency, not just recency.
